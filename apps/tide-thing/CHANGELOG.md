# tide-thing

## 0.1.1

First public version. Same app as the private 0.1.0 test build, plus the app icon.

- Four screens: Tide now, Moon & tide, Flood risk, On the water.
- NWS alert strip on every screen (red for warnings, amber otherwise), spoken warnings through the phone.
- US coasts only. Data from NOAA CO-OPS, NOAA NDBC and the National Weather Service.

Not tested: speech over the car speakers, the live NWS alert lookup, flood-level conversion at stations other than Charleston, SC.

# FAQ

**Is it safe to use for deciding whether to drive through water?** No. It is not for navigation or safety.

**Why does it say "Higher than usual" instead of "flood"?** Many stations have no official flood levels. Saying "flood" without them would be a guess.

**Does it work outside the US?** No. NOAA and NWS cover US coasts only.

**It says "No NOAA tide station within 150 miles".** The nearest NOAA water-level station is more than 150 miles away.

**Why is a number grayed out or amber?** The data is old. Amber after 30 minutes, flood wording grayed after 1 hour.

**Where do the alerts come from?** The National Weather Service. All severe alerts show, not just flood.

**It does not speak.** Spoken warnings go through the phone. Wheel click toggles them. A warning is spoken once.

**Does it cost anything or need a key?** No.

**Who wrote it?** Thomas Marlowe directed it; an AI tool wrote the code. See [how-this-was-built.md](how-this-was-built.md).
